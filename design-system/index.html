Design system

Design tokens

The foundation layer for every component on this site. Primitives hold the raw palette; semantic tokens are what components actually consume, so the system can be rethemed in one place. Every contrast ratio below is measured, not estimated.

Why these steps are spaced by L* rather than contrast ratio

Contrast ratio answers "can this text be read against that background". It says nothing about whether two dark fills look different to each other. The first version of the danger button moved from #9e241d to #8c1d18 on hover — a respectable-looking change on paper, but only 4.6 ΔL* in CIE lightness, which is at the edge of what anyone can perceive. In use the delete button appeared not to respond at all.

The interactive steps are now spaced by roughly 12–16 ΔL*, and every state pairs the colour change with a second cue: filled buttons lift 1px and gain a shadow on hover, then drop 1px and lose it on press. That 2px of travel is what makes the state readable on a small target, and it keeps working for anyone who cannot distinguish the two reds at all.

Adapting an inspiration palette for accessibility

The palette started from an inspiration palette: black, a run of grays, and a single muted teal. The teal measures 2.75:1 on the light base, below the 4.5:1 text floor, and the original sets white button text on it at 2.95:1. That is normal: inspiration palettes are chosen to look good as large blocks, not to carry words. Rather than abandon it, the teal keeps its original value and changes its job. Black text on it measures 7.13:1, so it fills the primary button and the brand colour becomes the thing you press. Darker steps take every link on a light surface, and a lighter step takes links on black.

The brand is monochrome by decision rather than by accident. Apart from the single teal, the four status colours are the only colour anywhere in the system — so when something does turn green or amber, it always means something.

Measure and leading

Body copy is capped at roughly 68 characters. Line length has more effect on reading comfort than font size does: too long and the eye loses its place returning to the next line, too short and the rhythm breaks. This paragraph sits at the default measure with normal leading, which is the setting used for every long-form passage on the site.

Radius and elevation

Shadows are true black at low opacity, so they lift a surface without adding a tint.

Motion and focus

Durations collapse to zero when the operating system requests reduced motion — handled at the token level so no component has to remember to.

Tab to the buttons to see the focus ring: 3px, offset by 2px, near-black on light surfaces and near-white on dark, so focus never looks like another shade of hover.
